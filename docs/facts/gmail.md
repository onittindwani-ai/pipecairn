# Gmail (sending through SMTP and reading through IMAP)

Checked 2026-11-22. https://support.google.com/mail

- VERIFIED: 2 step verification must be on, then make an app password at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
- IMAP imap.gmail.com port 993. SMTP smtp.gmail.com port 465 (SSL). Port 587 was not confirmed.
- Less secure app access ended on 2024-09-30 (third party source).
- Do not cold email from your main business domain or mailbox. Use separate domains. See docs/02.
